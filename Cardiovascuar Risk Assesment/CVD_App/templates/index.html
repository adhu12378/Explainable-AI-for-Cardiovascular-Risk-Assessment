System Capabilities

The proposed system combines predictive machine learning, explainable AI and clinical decision-support functionality.

AI

Risk Prediction

Machine learning models analyse patient characteristics to estimate the probability of cardiovascular disease.

SH

SHAP Explainability

SHAP-based explanations identify the patient characteristics that contribute most strongly to the prediction.

CF

What-If Analysis

Modify selected patient risk factors and observe how the predicted cardiovascular risk changes.

RS

Risk Stratification

Patients can be categorised into Low, Moderate, High and Very High cardiovascular risk levels.

FR

Fairness Evaluation

Model performance can be assessed across demographic groups to identify potential prediction disparities.

PC

Probability Calibration

Calibration analysis evaluates whether predicted probabilities correspond to observed outcomes.

How the System Works

The application follows a structured cardiovascular risk assessment workflow.

1

Patient Data

Enter demographic, physiological and lifestyle characteristics.

2

AI Prediction

The trained machine learning model calculates cardiovascular disease probability.

3

SHAP Explanation

The system identifies the most influential factors behind the prediction.

4

Decision Support

Risk information and preventive guidance are presented to support clinical assessment.

Clinical Decision Support Notice

This application is designed as a research prototype for cardiovascular disease risk assessment. Predictions and recommendations are intended to support, not replace, professional clinical judgement. The system should not be used as a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
