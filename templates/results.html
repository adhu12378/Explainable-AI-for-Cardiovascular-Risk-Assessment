Clinical Disclaimer

This system is an experimental Explainable AI Clinical Decision Support System developed for MSc dissertation research. The predicted probability and risk classification are generated by a machine learning model and should not be interpreted as a medical diagnosis. The system is intended to support, not replace, professional clinical judgement. Healthcare professionals should consider the patient's complete clinical history and appropriate clinical guidelines before making decisions.
